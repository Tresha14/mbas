## Collection, consent and storage

- Every recorded participant signed the consent screen (`meta.json -> consent`); sessions without consent are never imported for labelling.
- Personal data under India's DPDP Act 2023: raw sessions stay on project machines, are git-ignored and are deleted on request (see `docs/data_protocol.md`, section 6).
- Labels describe observable behaviour in a mock interview. They are not a judgement of the person, and model outputs trained on them are decision support only.

## Known limitations

- Webcam attention and engagement labels are subjective and culture-dependent (eye-contact norms); kappa is reported so readers can judge label reliability.
- Participants are mostly engineering students aged 18-25: results may not transfer to other groups.
- The synthetic dataset's relationships are designed; high scores on it mostly mean the simulator was learned.
